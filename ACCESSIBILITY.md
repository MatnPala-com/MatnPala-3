# Accessibility

At **MatnPala**, we believe that understanding text should be effortless for everyone. Accessibility is not an afterthought; it is a core part of our minimalist design philosophy. We are committed to making our platform usable by people with diverse abilities, ensuring that no one is left behind in the age of AI.

This document outlines our priorities, how we handle accessibility issues, and our current known limitations.

## Priorities

We strive to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** as our aspirational target. Our primary focus areas include:

- **Clarity & Simplicity:** Ensuring text contrast is high and the interface remains uncluttered to reduce cognitive load.
- **Keyboard Navigation:** Ensuring all core functionalities (summarizing, navigating, and accessing the library) can be performed using a keyboard.
- **Screen Reader Support:** Structuring our HTML semantically so assistive technologies can accurately parse and read the content.
- **Responsive Design:** Ensuring the platform remains fully functional on various screen sizes and zoom levels.

*Note: While we aim for these standards, MatnPala is an evolving project. We do not currently claim full, verified conformance with WCAG.*

## Contributor Expectations

If you are contributing code to MatnPala, we expect you to prioritize accessibility:

- **Semantic HTML:** Use appropriate HTML tags rather than relying solely on CSS or JavaScript for structure.
- **ARIA Attributes:** Use ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) attributes when building complex UI components (like modals or dropdowns).
- **Testing:** Before submitting a Pull Request with UI changes, please test your changes using a screen reader (like NVDA or VoiceOver) and keyboard-only navigation.

## Reporting Accessibility Issues

If you encounter a barrier while using MatnPala, we want to know. Please report it directly to us:

📧 **Email:** info@refinetext.ir

**What to include in your report:**
- A brief description of the task you were trying to complete.
- The specific URL or page where the issue occurred.
- The browser, operating system, and assistive technology you were using.
- A screenshot or recording (optional).

*You are not required to disclose a disability to report an accessibility issue. We value your privacy.*

### How we respond

We take these reports seriously. You can expect the following from us:
- **Acknowledgment:** We will reply to your email within **72 hours**.
- **Triage:** We will evaluate the issue and determine a course of action.
- **Resolution:** We will keep you updated on our progress and provide a fix as soon as reasonably possible.

## Known Limitations

We are currently aware of the following accessibility areas that need improvement:
- **Complex PDF Exports:** PDFs generated by the system may not be fully tagged for screen reader compatibility.
- **Dynamic Loading:** Some loading animations or real-time processing indicators may not be optimally announced by screen readers.

## Feedback and improvements

If you have suggestions on how we can improve our accessibility practices or this document, please reach out to us at **info@refinetext.ir**. We are always learning and iterating.
